# Region profiles

These follow common UK, US, European and Gulf careers advice. Most of it comes from careers blogs, so where an
employer or recruiter says otherwise, follow them. Outside the user's home country, write the phone number in
international format (+44 7700 900123, not 07700 900123).

- **UK and Ireland (the default when `cv-profile.md` names no home region)**
  - A "CV": A4, British English, 2 pages at most.
  - A short personal statement at the top.
  - No photo, date of birth, marital status or nationality.
  - Add a right-to-work line under the contact line only when the posting raises right to work or sponsorship and
    career-facts.md section 3 answers it.
- **US and Canada**
  - A "resume": `--letter` paper, 1 page if the must-haves fit, otherwise 2.
  - US spelling in the resume's own text ("optimization", "modeling"), so it matches the posting for applicant
    tracking systems.
  - No photo, date of birth, nationality or marital status (US employers avoid them for anti-discrimination
    reasons).
  - Contact line: city and country in place of a street address, plus phone, email and links.
  - Never state work authorisation. If the posting requires it and career-facts.md says the user needs
    sponsorship, flag it in the read-out as a likely rule-out.
- **Germany, Austria, Switzerland**
  - 1-2 pages, reverse-chronological, exact month and year on every date.
  - The CV's language follows the posting's.
  - A photo is common but optional: include one only if career-facts.md section 3 gives a photo file. The
    renderer cannot place images, so say so and render with `--docx` for the user to add it in Word.
  - A cover letter is expected: draft one (subject to tailoring rule 10).
- **Netherlands, the Nordics, the rest of Europe**
  - 2 pages, a direct tone, no photo unless career-facts.md gives one.
  - The Netherlands expects a short, direct motivation letter: draft one (subject to tailoring rule 10).
  - Use Europass only if the employer asks for it.
- **Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman)**
  - 2-3 pages.
  - A personal-details line under the contact line is expected: nationality, visa status, notice period or
    availability, date of birth, languages, driving licence. Fill it only from career-facts.md section 3, and list
    the blanks in `notes.md`.
  - A photo is common: see the note on photos above.
  - A languages line matters there.
- **Remote, or anywhere else:** use the profile of the employer's home country. If that is unclear, use the
  user's home region, and say which you chose.
