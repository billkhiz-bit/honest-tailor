Analytics Engineer
Northwind Health (fictional), London, hybrid (3 days a week in the office)

Closing date: 30 November 2026

into decisions for 300 branches.

What you'll do
- Build and own dbt models and Airflow pipelines on PostgreSQL and AWS
- Forecast stock demand so branches stop running out of high-demand medicines
- Build Power BI dashboards that branch managers actually use
- Automate the reporting our operations team still does by hand

What we're looking for (required)
- Strong SQL and Python
- dbt and Airflow in production
- Experience forecasting demand or stock
- Experience working with operations teams

Nice to have
- Logistics or delivery routing experience
- Snowflake
- Healthcare or pharmacy experience

How to apply
Send your CV and answer this question in under 200 words: "Tell us about a pipeline or model you built that
changed how an operations team works." We read every answer ourselves. AI-written answers will be deprioritised.
