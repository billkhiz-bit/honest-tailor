# Job applications

One row per application, newest first. Each folder holds the CV as sent, the posting, and notes (gaps, interview
prep, history). Kept up to date by the track-applications skill, or by editing this table directly.

**Statuses:** Tailored (not sent) · Applied · Interview (say which stage) · Offer · Rejected · Withdrawn · No reply.
Follow up once, 10 working days after applying (or a week after the closing date). Close as "No reply" four weeks
after the follow-up.
