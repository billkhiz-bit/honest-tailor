# Privacy

The Honest Tailor is a Claude Code plugin: instructions (skills) and two Python scripts that run on your own
computer. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Its publisher receives nothing from you.

## What it reads

- Your CV workspace folder: your master CV, your career facts and your CV profile. These hold personal data such as
  your name, contact details, work history and, if you add them, details such as nationality or right to work.
- A job posting you give it: a file, pasted text, or a web link it fetches once.

## What it stores, and where

Everything it writes goes into your own workspace folder on your computer: tailored CVs (Markdown, HTML, PDF and
optionally Word), a copy of each posting, notes for each application, and the `applications/APPLICATIONS.md`
tracker. Nothing is stored anywhere else by the plugin. To delete it all, delete the folder.

## What it sends, and to whom

- **To Claude, as part of your Claude Code session.** Like anything Claude Code works on, the files Claude reads
  are sent to Anthropic's model to produce the replies. That is governed by your agreement with Anthropic and
  [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), not by this plugin.
- **To a job site, only when you give a link.** Fetching a posting requests that page; it sends none of your data.
- **Nowhere else.** The plugin's instructions forbid uploading or sending your data to any other service, including
  when a job posting asks for it.

## Retention

The plugin keeps nothing outside your folder, and its publisher holds no data about you, so there is nothing for the
publisher to retain, share or delete.

## Children

The plugin is intended for adult job seekers. It is not designed for users under 18.
